# zz-medieval-weapons

Zee's Medieval Weapons: ten historical weapons on gs-weapon, as a Workshop pack (`provides.json`, games `*`). Read the family [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md), [gs-weapon's](../gs-weapon/CLAUDE.md) and [README.md](README.md). Modelled on zee-gs-weapons, with its own minimal view model, world model and replication (it does not depend on zee-gs-weapons): catalogue rows, behaviours, config, models, a spawn provider and `view/`, `net/`; the detail is in [docs/view-model.md](docs/view-model.md).

- Tuning is in weapon profiles `cfg/addons/zz_medieval_weapons/weapon/<id>.yml` (`GSWeaponProfiles` in gs-weapon over gs-core's `GSProfileSet`; pack keys under `extra:`, `upgrades/` children in the folder form), with the old `*.yml` files kept as a top layer; `ZzMedievalConfig.defaults()` is the source of truth and the shipped yml and `weapons/*.tres` are **generated** by `tools/export-defs.sh`. Change a default, re-run it, commit all of it.
- Behaviours apply nothing: stamina and knockback are outcome events the game applies; blocking is `ZzMedievalBlock`, a pure function.
- Durations are ticks at 64 (`authored_tick_rate`).
- Models: `assets/<id>.glb` if present, else a primitive placeholder (every weapon has a vendored model; `view/zz_medieval_reflection_source.gd` and docs/lighting.md say what a view gives metal to reflect; see CREDITS.md). Credit every vendored model there and in the README.
- A spawn menu consumes `spawn_provider.gd`; do not copy it into a game. It also has `weapon_art()` (`art/zz_medieval_zee_art.gd`: zee's `ZeeWeaponArt` per id on these models, by global-class lookup so no zee class is named; empty without zee) and `model_paths()`; `ZzMedievalModels.model_path` finds `assets/` beside a mounted pack too. `tools/art_sheet.sh` renders the bare stand-in beside it (zee linked in, dev only).
- `ZzMedievalArtTable` is the only file that knows how a weapon is held and moves; `ZzWeaponAnim` holds no nodes and reads no input. A lambda connected to an anim must not capture the anim (a reference cycle, caught by the suite's leak check).
- Addon dependencies: the first-person hands and sleeves use gs-player-char (`GSViewHand`, its grip table; the QA figure is `GSHumanoidFigure`), loaded by path. It is linked from `.gitignore` (bootstrap derives links from it; gs-player-char brings gs-animation itself) and named for the published `requires.json` in `requires.uses.json`, because `GSAddonApi.derive` skips the pack's own `addons/` scripts. The shell never bundles it; the pack overlay delivers it. Add any further addon the pack's scripts load to both files.
- Check: `./game.sh test`; render with `tools/screenshot.sh` (`--sheet`, `--world`) and look at the frames.
- A delivered pack ships its profiles: `export_to` writes the `cfg/addons/<addon>/weapon` tree (upgrades included) under the export root. After mounting, set `ZzMedievalConfig.pack_cfg_root` to `GSCloudMounter.cfg_root_of(key)`; it is the weakest config root, so `cfg/addons`, `cfg/game` and `cfg/server` still override it. No gs-core change was needed (`GSProfileSet.roots` is weakest first).

## Visual quality

A visual change (a weapon model, its animation, arms or effects) is done only when a fresh judge says so: follow gs-game's brief, `../gs-game/docs/visual-judge-brief.md` (GPU capture, never llvmpipe; three consecutive zero-defect passes; `--audit` before you report). Your own reading of the sheets never counts.
